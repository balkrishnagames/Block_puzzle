Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Balkrishna Games ("we", "us", "our") built the Block Puzzle Master mobile game as an ad-supported game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide (optional): If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games / Google account for the leaderboard, we receive your player identifier and display name. We do not receive your password.

b) Automatically collected information: Device model, Android version, language, approximate country, and gameplay statistics (scores, levels, sessions). This is used in aggregate to improve the game.

c) Local storage: Your game progress, high scores, coins and settings are stored locally on your device. Clearing app data removes them.

2. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — serves advertisements. AdMob may use your advertising ID to show personalised or non-personalised ads, measure ad performance and detect fraud.
    Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Google Play In-App Review — used to ask for your rating of the game. No personal data is shared with us through this flow.
  • Google Firebase — used for crash reporting and anonymous analytics to keep the game stable.
    Firebase privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate the game, save your progress and maintain leaderboards
  • To display advertisements that keep the game free
  • To fix crashes and improve gameplay features
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or request: your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone/camera access, phone numbers, financial information, or health data.

5. Children's Privacy

The game is family friendly and rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The ads served are non-personalised for users in regions where personalised ads are restricted. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you signed in for leaderboards and want your cloud data deleted, contact us (see below) and we will remove it within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA and others) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these, contact us by email.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
