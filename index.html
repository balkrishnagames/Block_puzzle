About

Love block puzzles? Meet BlockForge — the endlessly satisfying puzzle game that's easy to learn and impossible to put down! Drag block pieces onto the 8×8 board. Fill a row or column to clear it. Chain clears to build combos — GOOD! EXCELLENT! AMAZING! — and beat your best score. No timer. No pressure. Just you, the board, and one more piece.

  • ★ Simple & relaxing — one-finger drag-and-drop controls
  • ★ Endless — every game is different; no two boards are alike
  • ★ Juicy combos — streak multipliers up to ×8, all-clear bonuses, praise celebrations
  • ★ 3 gorgeous themes — Night Candy, Sunset, Ocean
  • ★ Smart rescue — continue after game over, undo tricky moves
  • ★ Offline — play anywhere, no wifi needed
