Account & Data Deletion

BlockForge has no accounts

BlockForge does not offer account creation or sign-in, so there is no server-side account or profile to delete. We do not collect or store your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information on our servers — we don't operate any game servers at all.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, selected theme, and sound setting are saved locally on your device only. To delete this data:

  • Uninstall the app — removes all locally stored data immediately, or
  • Android Settings → Apps → BlockForge → Storage & cache → Clear storage — resets scores and preferences without uninstalling.

Advertising data

BlockForge shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect an advertising identifier as described in our Privacy Policy. This identifier is controlled by your device, not by us — you can reset it or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Settings → Google → Ads on your device.
